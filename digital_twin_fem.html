The disc heats up from 2 sources, each with its OWN speed:
① Foucault eddy currents (~4K) induced in the disc — appear instantly with I².
② Hot air from the coils (~8K) — coils sit only 3.8mm below the disc. This part is slow: the copper mass takes ~25min to warm, so the bottom air lags behind I².
Total ΔT_ss ≈ 11K — hot air is the dominant (but delayed) heat source for the disc.
